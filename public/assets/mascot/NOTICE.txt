The title creatures
===================

The creatures worn with a title (public/js/mascots/: the minnow, the electric ray, the
golden whale and those that follow) are original to Synapsez, drawn in code for it in
November 2026. They share the general style of pixel mascots like the one above (flat
colours, a one-pixel outline) but no pixel, shape, prop or frame was copied from these
pictures.
