        Preferences

        For models reached through OpenRouter. Strict sends requests only to zero-data-retention endpoints that neither store nor train on what you send. Many free models are not served that way, so they will stop working while this is on.

        Email addresses, phone numbers, ID and bank-account numbers, card numbers, and a name written after "my name is" are replaced with placeholders before anything is sent, and put back in the reply and before any tool runs. A PDF goes as its text while this is on. Pictures and scanned pages go as they are, and a name in the middle of a sentence, an address, or anything identifying by context is not caught. The model works with the placeholders, so answers about those details can be less precise.

        Conversations not continued for this long are deleted with their files. Pinned conversations are always kept.

        On this deployment, page views and page speed are counted by Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights: no cookies, only the page's address without anything after ? or #, never what you write. A browser set to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is not counted.

        Memory

        What the assistant remembers between conversations, and what it may look back at. All of it stays in this app's database; it reaches the model you chose only as part of a conversation — and that model's provider handles it under its own terms. When the assistant changes a note, what it said before is kept so you can undo the change; editing or deleting the note yourself removes it.

        When you mention something discussed before, the assistant may look it up. Inside a project it searches only that project; incognito conversations are never searched.

        Saved notes are read at the start of each conversation, and new ones are saved when something is worth keeping. Off: nothing is read or saved, and the notes stay until you delete them.

        Health, religion, politics, ethnicity and sexuality are not saved unless this is on. Identity, passport, tax, bank and card numbers, criminal records and immigration status are never saved.

        Bring memory from another assistant

        Ask Claude or ChatGPT to "write out everything you remember about me", paste the answer here, and it becomes one note you can read and edit below.
