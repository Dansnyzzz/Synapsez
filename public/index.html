    Your data

    Security activity

    Sign-ins, password and two-factor changes, keys, exports and shared links on this account — never what was said. Addresses are kept only to their network. Kept for 180 days.

    Delete account

    Deletes the account and everything in it — conversations, files, notes, projects, keys and tasks — at once and for good. Export your data first if you want a copy.

    People

    Anyone can sign up with an email and a password. Each account brings its own API keys and pairs its own computer — never yours. Set ALLOW_SIGNUP=false to close registration once everyone you want is in.
